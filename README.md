# UdyamSetu 🌉

### KNOW THE MARKET. STRUCTURE THE CAPITAL. BUILD SMARTER.

UdyamSetu is an AI-assisted, hyper-local business advisory and financial structuring platform designed to help rural micro-entrepreneurs make informed business decisions.

It helps entrepreneurs understand **what business may work in their location, whether the idea is feasible, how much capital is required, which financing or government schemes may be relevant, and what steps to take next.**
